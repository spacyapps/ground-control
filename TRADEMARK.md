## What a fork may and may not do

**May:**

- fork, build, run, and redistribute the **code** under the AGPL;
- copy `theme.json`, `Themes/default/` and `Themes/example-avatars/` as a
  reference for the theme format and as a minimal starting skin — these are
  configuration and placeholder assets, not SpacyApps artwork;
- refer to this project by name to say what your fork is based on ("a fork of
  Ground Control by SpacyApps") — nominative use is fine.

**May not, without written permission:**

- call the fork "Ground Control", or publish it under the "SpacyApps" name, or
  use either name or a confusingly similar one as your product's name, icon, or
  domain;
- ship any SpacyApps theme artwork or character (Lunar Avatar and the rest), or
  a modified version of it, or a new depiction of the same character.

A fork must ship under its own name and with its own art.
